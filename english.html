Welcome to Hebi Huakang Nephrology Specialty Hospital

A second-level TCM nephrology specialty hospital in Henan Province

Hebi Huakang Nephrology Specialty Hospital (formerly Qixian Huakang Hospital), founded in October 2010, is a second-level TCM nephrology specialty hospital approved by the Hebi Municipal Health Commission, a designated medical insurance hospital and a designated cross-province settlement unit for national medical insurance. The hospital is also a designated remote consultation hospital of PLA General Hospital (Beijing 301) and Peking Union Medical College Hospital, a supported unit of Peking University First Hospital, and a collaborative hospital with the First Affiliated Hospital of Henan University of Chinese Medicine.

Focusing on kidney disease treatment with TCM characteristics, the hospital provides inpatient treatment, health guidance and dialysis services for patients with chronic kidney diseases. Its proprietary TCM preparations and the "Yin-Yang Balance and Immune Repair Dual Therapy" (awarded the Henan Provincial Science and Technology Progress Award in 2005) are core specialties.
